💡 How to play

Think like a park manager.

1. Set admission. High ticket prices earn more per guest but can keep families away.

2. Price rides and food. Guests are more likely to buy when prices feel fair. Every sale has a cost.

3. Choose marketing. More advertising can increase attendance, but it comes straight out of your bank.

4. Watch cash flow. Profit is added to your bank balance. If your bank runs out, keep prices and marketing sustainable.

5. Play 30 days. Build your bank balance and compare daily reports to learn what works.
